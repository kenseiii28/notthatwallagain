How to Play ▾
  1. Goal: Be the first pawn to reach any square on your opponent’s baseline (opposite edge).
  2. On your turn: Either move your pawn one square (orthogonally), or place one wall.
  3. Walls: Spans two squares wide. Blocks pawns from crossing. Each player starts with 10 walls.
  4. Fairness rule: You can never place a wall that completely traps a player — at least one valid path must remain open.
