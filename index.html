Guide

How to Play Not That Wall Again

A minimalist board game of speed, spite, and spatial navigation. Race your pawn to the opposite baseline while dropping well-timed barricades in the computer's path.

🎯

Objective

Be the first pawn to reach any square on the opponent's baseline (Row 9 / North for you; Row 1 / South for the computer).

♟️

Pawn Movement

On your turn, move your pawn one square orthogonally (North, South, East, West) to an unblocked adjacent square.

🦘

Jumping Opponents

When adjacent to the opponent, jump straight over them. If a wall or board edge blocks the straight jump, leap diagonally around them to an open side.

🧱

Wall Placement

Instead of moving, place one wall. Walls span two squares wide (horizontal or vertical) in the grooves between cells to block pawn movement.

🔟

10-Wall Limit

Each player begins with 10 walls. Once your inventory is depleted, you may only move your pawn.

⚖️

The Golden Rule

Never trap a player. You cannot place a wall that completely blocks either player's path to their goal. At least one open route must always remain.
